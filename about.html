Who We Are

Helping Every Learner Discover Their Potential

Vessel of Honour School is a learning community focused on creating meaningful educational experiences for children.

We believe that every learner has unique abilities, interests and potential. Our responsibility is to provide the guidance, encouragement and environment that helps those qualities develop.

Education, in our view, is about more than academic performance. It is also about confidence, responsibility, relationships, curiosity and character.

  • Learner-centred experiences
  • Character and personal development
  • Supportive learning environment
  • Strong school-family partnership

Educational Philosophy

Developing the Whole Child

We believe meaningful education should help learners develop academically while also building the personal qualities they need for life.

Our working philosophy places emphasis on curiosity, confidence, communication, collaboration, creativity and responsibility.

  • Curiosity and independent thinking
  • Communication and collaboration
  • Confidence and resilience
  • Creativity and practical learning
  • Responsibility and positive behaviour
